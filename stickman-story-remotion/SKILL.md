# stickman-story — Remotion stickman documentary starter

A simplified starter kit for stickman documentary videos built with
Remotion (React, TypeScript), by an Indonesian author; its README.id.md
is the Indonesian readme.

## Where things live

- Local checkout: `~/workspace/tools/stickman-story`
  (upstream: github.com/ahmadaryanto/stickman-story).
- Read first, in the checkout: `README.id.md` / `README.md`, then
  `CLAUDE.md` (the upstream author's own working notes for an agent),
  and `docs/`.
- Stack: `remotion.config.ts`, `src/` compositions, `public/` assets.

## How to work

1. Copy the checkout into the project's own folder; never build a
   deliverable inside the tools checkout.
2. Pair with the house `remotion` and `remotion-best-practices`
   skills for rendering discipline, and with `motion-design` for
   directing timing and emotion before writing scenes.
3. Narration, captions, and loudness follow the same standards as
   the house `stick-figure-video` skill unless the user says
   otherwise. Verify final frames personally before delivering.

## Fit and limits

- The proven house pipeline for stick-figure story shorts is
  `stick-figure-video`; this kit is an alternative engine
  (Remotion-native, documentary format), banked for comparison and
  for jobs that want a real Remotion project skeleton.
- UNTESTED here as of banking (21 Nov 2026): prove install, render,
  and export on a small scene before committing it to a deadline.
